El Arca Honduras

Institutional website for El Arca Honduras, a non-profit organization that has been building communities for people with and without intellectual disabilities since 1977. Features include a blog with MDX content, photo gallery, community pages, donation integration, SEO optimization, and full bilingual support (EN/ES). Built with Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Playwright for E2E testing.
